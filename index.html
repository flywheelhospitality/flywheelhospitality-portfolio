Who We Are

Built by Hospitality Professionals. Driven by Hotel Performance.

  • 2025 — Kallakurichi. Flywheel Hospitality was founded in Kallakurichi, starting with the operation of an 11-key property. What began as a focused hotel operations venture has grown into a broader hospitality management platform.
  • 2026 — Attur & Salem. Flywheel expanded its operating portfolio with 19 new keys in Attur and 21 keys in Salem, strengthening our presence across key hospitality markets in Tamil Nadu.

Behind Flywheel is a team of hospitality professionals with around 20 years of combined industry experience, bringing practical knowledge across hotel operations, front office, housekeeping, revenue management, OTA distribution, sales, marketing and guest experience.

We believe hotel management is not simply about keeping a property running. It is about building the right systems, making better commercial decisions, creating consistent guest experiences and helping owners achieve sustainable business performance.
