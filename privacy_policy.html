Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Flywheel Hospitality respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when applying for employment opportunities with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Flywheel Hospitality collects, uses, stores and protects information submitted by candidates through our recruitment forms, including Facebook and Instagram lead forms.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a job application or recruitment enquiry, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or location
  • Education information
  • Job title or current/previous role
  • Work experience
  • Notice period
  • Expected salary
  • Other information voluntarily provided during the recruitment process

We only request information that is relevant to evaluating and contacting candidates for employment opportunities.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you regarding the job opportunity you applied for
  • Review your suitability for the position
  • Schedule interviews or further discussions
  • Communicate regarding the recruitment process
  • Maintain recruitment records
  • Contact you regarding other relevant employment opportunities where appropriate

We will not use the information submitted through this recruitment form for unrelated marketing purposes without an appropriate basis or consent where required.

4. Information Collected Through Facebook and Instagram

If you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram recruitment form, the information you provide may be shared with Flywheel Hospitality so that we can process your application.

Your information may initially be collected through Meta's platform and subsequently provided to Flywheel Hospitality for recruitment purposes.

5. Sharing of Information

Flywheel Hospitality may share candidate information only where reasonably necessary for recruitment and business operations, such as with authorized personnel involved in the hiring process or service providers assisting with recruitment administration.

We do not sell candidate personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect candidate information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain candidate information only for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, employment-related purposes, record keeping, or as required by applicable law.

If you are not selected for a particular position, we may retain your information for consideration for future relevant opportunities, subject to applicable requirements.

8. Your Requests

You may contact Flywheel Hospitality to request information about the personal data we hold about you or to request correction or deletion of your information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

For privacy-related requests, please contact Flywheel Hospitality through the contact details provided on our official business channels.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Flywheel Hospitality may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our recruitment practices, business operations or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
